1. Executive Summary & Business Objective

Traditional enterprise LMS builds require months of software development, outsourced voice talent, and heavy administrative overhead. To eliminate training latency and scale the PHL outbound desk immediately, this platform was architected and built using full-stack rapid application generation integrated directly with high-tier speech engines.

The primary objective is to fast-track agent competency across three core operational barriers: Australian dialect and accent familiarity, spatial/technical credibility on QLD residential roofs, and high-pressure objection handling against unscripted prospects.

Module 2: Cold Calling Masterclass · Appointment Setter Scripts LIVE

  • Feature: The official Solar Surge Setter Playbook (8 Oct 2026), built into the LMS word for word. It is the single source for the script, qualifying rules and rebuttals across every module, the practice calls, the AI coaches and QA scoring.
  • Call flow (Appointment Setter Scripts, 14 Oct 2026): followed word for word, in order: the opener for the lead source (call centre, online enquiry or door-to-door) → Q1 the highest bill in the last 12 months → the free property assessment (45 minutes to an hour with everyone who owns and lives in the home, then a quote) → the qualifying block (Q2–Q6) → one specific time for both of them → lock it in (both there for the full hour, the summer bill handy). Confirmers follow the Confirm Script (14 Oct): opening, re-jog, details, the same six questions, then lock it in, or the holding line on any flagged answer.
  • Six qualifiers: the highest bill ($450+ per quarter or $150+ per month), both decision-makers there for the full hour, year of birth (65–70 flagged for manager review, over 70 not booked), staying 3+ years, employment (recorded), and the roof and renovations (minor repairs flagged, major work means a call back). Each answer is marked Book it, Flag to manager or Don't book, automatically.
  • Rebuttals: 13 objections, each with a one-liner and a full script, handled Acknowledge → Reframe → Ask → Advance (twice at most). Reps never quote prices or ballparks, never book a one-legger, and only use urgency that is true.
  • Rebuttals in reach: a floating “Rebuttals” button and the R key open the floor quick-reference card on every page; type what the homeowner said and the script appears. During practice calls the matching rebuttal pins itself automatically.
  • Business Impact: One method for every rep, coach and AI, so appointments are fully qualified, remembered and kept.

2a. Sales Dojo: scripts and rebuttals at a glance (added 9 Oct) LIVE

  • Purpose: One dedicated page, first in every rep's menu, for finding and reading any script mid-call. Large type throughout, with a text-size control so every rep can read comfortably at a glance.
  • Search: Reps type what the homeowner said (“talk to my wife”, “how much”, “email me”) and the right rebuttal appears with its one-liner and full script.
  • Step-by-step call guide: Eight steps from the Setter Playbook, one at a time: open with rapport (by lead source) → qualify as a two-way conversation (all six qualifiers, each showing what the homeowner usually says back and the follow-up) → reflect → position the assessment → offer two times → lock it in → set expectations → log the booking.
  • Setting expectations: The homeowner leaves the call knowing what happens next: a text with the consultant's name, a confirmation email to review before the visit, and a list of questions to prepare so the consultant covers them during the visit. This supports Solar Surge's goal of being the homeowner's most trusted educator on their options, including the government incentives available to them.
  • Script tailored to each lead (added 9 Oct): Reps paste the lead details from any of our vendors, in whatever format the vendor uses (e.g. “Has Solar, 36 Panels, 8–9 Years Old, No Battery, Home Owner, Power Bills $900/quarter, Call Back Morning”). In a few seconds the AI reads it and rewrites the whole call script for that homeowner: the opener and pre-call text, the qualifying questions (anything the vendor already captured becomes “I've got here that…” to verify; anything missing stays a question), the reflect line and the close. It also shows a lead summary, the six qualifiers as vendor-confirmed, ruled out or still to ask, and practical watch-outs (e.g. a requested call-back time, a bill below the minimum with the polite exit line, older panels and feed-in tariffs). The bill check always applies the official $450 a quarter / $150 a month minimum, and no prices are ever added to the script. Lead details are not saved; they stay in the rep's browser only.
  • “Where are you located?” answered locally (added 9 Oct): Reps paste the customer's address from our records. The app finds it (using the Queensland Government address database) and works out which part of our service area the customer is in and how far they are from our Coomera head office. It then gives a ready-made answer to this common brush-off: acknowledge that they want a proper local business, explain that we are Queensland-based with consultants across South East Queensland including their area (mentioning the distance only when it's close), ask whether having someone local for the warranty matters to them, then get straight back to the call. Customers outside our usual area (e.g. Toowoomba, Lismore, northern NSW, regional QLD) are flagged so the rep checks with the team before booking. Reps are reminded to be honest if asked where they personally are. The same answer appears when a rep searches “where are you located”. Nothing is saved.
  • Call notes in one standard format (added 9 Oct): Accurate post-call notes are one of our biggest gaps, and we need them consistent in Zoho. Next to the script, setters now have a Call notes panel (the Sales Dojo shows three columns on a wide screen: the customer’s lead and address, the script, and the notes; as the rep moves through the script, the matching note boxes light up, and each qualifying question in the script has an “Asked” tick linked to the notes): an outcome (booked, call back, disqualified, not interested, no answer), the appointment time and consultant, and every qualifying question written out: the rep ticks it once asked, and a note box opens for what the customer said, plus existing solar, objections, what the customer wants the consultant to look at, a personal detail, and whether the pre-call text was sent. Reps can type fast with shorthand and typos; one click and the AI tidies the wording (without adding anything the rep didn't write), turns dates like “tmrw 6pm” into the real day and date, states whether the customer is Qualified or Not qualified with the reasons (all six qualifiers must be asked and pass, as the playbook requires; a booking that isn’t qualified is flagged for the team lead), then the app lays it out in one fixed standard format: header, outcome, the six qualifiers (any not asked are clearly marked “NOT ASKED”, and the bill is checked against the $450/$150 minimum), then the details. The rep copies it into Aircall in one click, so every note that reaches Zoho reads the same way. The panel also reminds the rep which qualifiers are still to ask before they hang up. Nothing is saved in the LMS.
  • Yes/No answers and a bill calculator (added 9 Oct): Each qualifying question in the script and the notes now has Yes / No buttons (worded so Yes always means the customer meets the qualifier) and a small details box. The bill takes the amount and Monthly or Quarterly, and instantly shows both figures (e.g. 50 a month = 50 a quarter) with a clear “meets / below the minimum” check, so reps never need a calculator. Working status is recorded as Full-time, Part-time or Retired; for retired homeowners the pension type and over/under 65 must be recorded, for audit purposes and because different programs apply. Qualified / Not qualified is now decided exactly from these answers.
  • Customer name, bill and appointment time (added 12 Oct): Reps paste the customer's name (and partner's) from Zoho into a name box, and it appears throughout the script and in the notes. When the lead notes mention a bill, the AI fills in the bill box automatically (monthly or quarterly as the lead says; if the lead doesn't say which, the rep picks), so the rep can read it back and confirm it with the customer; the rep can always change it, and the notes record whether the bill came from the lead notes or Zoho. Booked and call-back times are picked from a calendar and a time list that are always in Queensland time, whatever the rep's computer is set to, so reps in Manila can't book the wrong hour; each time also shows the Manila equivalent for the rep's reference.
  • Setter or Confirmer script (added 13 Oct): Before starting, each rep picks the calls they're making from an “I'm calling as” dropdown: Setter (booking new appointments, the Setter Call Flow V3) or Confirmer (confirming booked appointments, the official Confirm Script, word for word). AI Drew then shows only that role's script, so every rep follows the right one. The Confirmer script runs in six steps: open, confirm the appointment, re-jog their memory, both present with a bill ready, confirm the details (bill, working or retired, pension, over or under 65, moving soon, roof condition), and lock it in. The confirmer's call note uses the same standard layout, showing the appointment on file, the outcome (Confirmed, Rescheduled, Cancelled, No answer or Call back) and the details re-confirmed; it warns if a re-confirmed detail fails (for example the customer is over 70) before the consultant goes out. Rescheduling uses the same Queensland-time picker and field rep calendar; cancelling needs a reason from the Zoho list. In the Admin Control Centre (People), admins can set each person's usual script, so AI Drew opens on it; reps can still switch for the day, for example a setter covering confirm calls when someone is on leave, and go back to their usual script the next day.
  • Drafts awaiting approval: The confirmation email, the homeowner prep questions and the “educator” lines are new and not yet in the official playbook. They are clearly marked “Draft” in the app until approved (see section 6). They name incentive programs but never quote dollar figures.

6a. Built-in AI QA Coach Framework (added 7 Oct)

  • Every call is scored against Solar Surge's AI QA Coach Framework (Solar Appointment Setting Evaluation Model), built into the app: four areas worth 25 points each, adding up to a QA score out of 100.
  • The AI also uses Solar Surge's company knowledge (products, service areas, qualification rules).
  • Coaching reads like a human coach wrote it. The framework guides what the AI looks for, but the coaching form never shows framework labels or jargon. It reads as personal notes from a coach to a rep: what went well, pivot points with suggested lines, and clear next steps.
  • Every coaching form ends with a practice prompt sending the rep to a 10-minute session with Sharon, the AI customer.
  • “Rewrite coaching notes with AI” lets QA regenerate a draft before publishing.

6a-i. Setters and Confirmers, with a Qualification Check (added 9 Oct)

  • Two rep types: Before scoring a call, the coach chooses whether the rep is an Appointment Setter or a Confirmer. Setters are judged against the official Solar Surge Setter Playbook; Confirmers against the official Solar Surge Confirm Script (CC, July 2026), both built into the app word for word. The QA score stays out of 100.
  • Every qualifying question, every call: The AI checks the transcript against the full list of questions for that rep type and marks each one as passed, failed, missed or not applicable, quoting the call.
    • Setters (6): homeowner with every owner present; bill $450+ a quarter or $150+ a month; shading; working status; staying put 2–3 years; roof and insurance. Plus pension type and age if retired.
    • Confirmers: appointment time confirmed; both owners there for the full hour; bill ready; full name; partner's name; address; average bill; working or retired; not moving in 2–3 years; roof condition; insurance work or roof damage; pension type and age if retired; locked in with expectations set.
    Any question the rep missed is listed automatically on the coaching form as a script deviation.
  • Qualified or not: Each coaching form shows whether the customer is Qualified, Not qualified (an answer rules them out, with the deciding quote) or Not verified (a qualifying question was missed, so it can't be confirmed). The verdict follows one fixed rule, so it is consistent from call to call. It appears on the coaching form, the records list, the rep's own records and the PDF.
  • Business Impact: Protects consultant time from unqualified visits, and makes skipping a qualifying question visible on every coached call.

6b. QA Document Uploads (added 7 Oct)

  • QA can upload reference documents (PDF, Word or text) that the AI reads and uses when writing the coaching form.
  • Campaign library: documents shared across every call in a campaign (scripts, product updates, rebuttal guides).
  • Per call / per form: documents attached to one specific call or coaching form.
  • QA can review and edit the text the AI will read before saving. Scanned or image-only PDFs can't be read.

6c. Agents Without a Login (added 7 Oct)

  • QA can add an agent by name directly in the QA & Coaching tool, without an email address or an account in the Admin Control Centre.
  • These agents are marked “(no login)”. Their calls are scored and coached the same way; their coaching forms are shared as a PDF, since they can't sign in to acknowledge them.
  • Duplicate names are detected, and an agent with existing calls or forms can't be deleted by accident.

4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

  • Onboarding Acceleration: Reduce ramp-to-floor time from a standard 14 days down to 3 days.
  • Lead Pipeline Quality: Eliminate low-value appointments by embedding the Setter Playbook's six qualifiers (including the $450 a quarter / $150 a month bill minimum) into rep workflows.
  • Dial Efficiency: Achieve a target benchmark booking rate of 0.016 per dial on live QLD data.
  • QA Coverage: Transition from ~5% manual call sampling to 100% automated AI call evaluation.

Reps can log several objections per call. Each one appears in the call note, so the team can see exactly what customers push back on and how it was handled.
