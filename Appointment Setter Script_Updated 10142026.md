# Appointment Setter Scripts — Solar Surge

Oct 14, 2026 · @Emil Halili

## Why we qualify this strictly

Every booking must pass all six qualifiers, because a bad booking costs a consultant hours of unpaid driving. Our consultants are independent contractors with no retainer and no fuel pay. A partner who isn't home, a bill under the minimum, a family moving next year, or a roof about to be replaced all mean a wasted trip, and that wrecks morale fast.

Use "what and why" on every question: tell the customer what you're asking and why it matters to them. Be direct and keep it moving. When people understand the reason, they answer honestly and turn up ready

## Qualifier quick reference

Ask these in order on every call. Anything in the "Flag" column gets booked but marked for manager review; anything in "Don't book" ends the booking.

| # | Question | Book it | Flag to manager | Don't book |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Highest bill in last 12 months (summer) | $150+/month or $450+/quarter | — | Under $150/month or $450/quarter |
| 2 | Both occupants / decision-makers attend | Both confirmed for the full 45–60 min | — | Only one can attend: rebook to a time both can do |
| 3 | Year of birth | Born 1962 or later (under 65) | Born 1956–1961 (65 to 70) | Born 1955 or earlier (over 70)\* |
| 4 | Moving plans | 3+ years | — | 0–3 years |
| 5 | Employment status | Record: full-time, part-time, casual or business owner | — | — |
| 6 | Roof, repairs, renovations | Roof OK, no major work on | Minor repairs: note details | Major work underway or planned: call back after |

\*Born 1955 but birthday hasn't happened yet this year means still 70, so treat as Flag. Year brackets are set for 2026; update them each January.

## Qualifying block (Q2 to Q6, all scripts)

Run these in order after the pitch and before you offer a time. Each one opens with what you're asking and closes with why.

Awesome. Our consultant is driving out to your area for this, so before I lock a time in I've got five quick questions. I'll tell you why I'm asking each one as we go, so we get it right the first time and don't waste your time.

### Q2: Both decision-makers present

Who else owns and lives at the property with you? … Great. Our consultants only run the assessment when everyone who owns and lives in the home is sitting in, the whole 45 minutes to an hour. The system gets sized on both your energy habits, and if there's any paperwork or finance, it needs both of you. So can I count on both you and \[partner name\] being there?

*Get a clear yes. If one can't make it: "No worries, when are you both usually home together, evenings or weekends?" and book that time. Never book with one decision-maker.*

### Q3: Year of birth

Next one: what year were you born? The reason I ask is the finance options our consultant can show you have age-based lending criteria, so this makes sure they only bring options you can actually use.

*Ask for the year, never the age. Born 1962 or later, continue. Born 1956 to 1961, continue, write "AGE REVIEW" in notes and flag to your manager before it's confirmed. Born 1955 or earlier, don't book (see Polite exits).*

### Q4: Moving plans

How long are you planning to stay in the home: 0 to 3 years, or 3 years or longer? I ask because these systems pay themselves off over a few years, and we want to make sure you're there to get the benefit.

*3+ years, continue. 0 to 3 years, don't book (see Polite exits).*

### Q5: Employment

Are you working full-time, part-time, casual, or do you run your own business? That just helps the consultant understand when you're home using power and which payment options suit.

*Record the answer exactly as given. Not a pass/fail question.*

### Q6: Roof and renovations

Last one, and it matters because the panels go on your roof. How's the roof: any leaks, damage, or repairs needed? Any insurance claims open? And is there any big work happening or planned, like a kitchen or bathroom reno?

*If anything comes up, dig in: What's the work? Has it started? When is it booked to finish? Is the roof being repaired or replaced? Has the insurance claim settled, and is it a cash settlement? Major work underway or planned, don't book now and schedule a call back for when it's done. Minor repairs, record the details and continue.*

## Polite exits and call backs

### Doesn't qualify (Q1 bill, Q3 over 70, Q4 moving within 3 years)

Thanks so much for running through that with me. Based on what you've told me, the options we offer aren't going to be the right fit for you right now, so I won't book you in and waste your time. Really appreciate your time, have a great day.

*Never tell the customer the specific reason they didn't qualify, especially age. Record the reason in the CRM notes.*

### Major work underway (Q6)

Sounds like you've got a fair bit on with the \[roof / reno\]. Rather than book something that clashes with that, when's it likely to wrap up? I'll make a note to give you a call once it's done.

*Within 3 weeks: Call Back stage. Longer than 3 weeks: Future Contact stage with an activity set for the date they gave.*

### Partner can't attend (Q2)

No problem. When are you both usually home together, evenings or weekends? Let's lock in a time that works for both of you.
