## What Is It

EV Solutions pairs a smart EV charger with the customer's existing or new solar system, turning their roof into a petrol station. The result: charging a vehicle from free solar energy instead of paying for petrol or grid electricity.

Solar-charged EV driving costs approximately **$1 per 100km** — compared to $15–$20+ for petrol.

EV Solutions is designed to be an **integrated part of the full energy ecosystem** — not a standalone wall plug. Without proper integration, an EV charger can overwhelm the switchboard, draw from the grid instead of solar, or create demand spikes that cost money.

**Key stats:**
- ~$1 per 100km when charged from solar
- 100% in-house licensed electrical teams for every install
- V2H (Vehicle-to-Home) ready — future-proofed for when this technology arrives in Australia
- Full site assessment and switchboard upgrade (if needed) included

## The Core Concept: Your Roof Is Your Petrol Station

Most EV owners charge from the grid by default — paying electricity rates rather than fuel rates, which is cheaper than petrol but not free. When an EV charger is integrated with a solar system, the car charges from free solar energy during the day.

**The problem with a standard wall plug:**
- Very slow charging
- No integration with solar — draws from grid indiscriminately
- Can create demand spikes that appear on electricity bills
- May overwhelm older switchboards without assessment

**The Solar Surge difference:** Every EV installation is engineered as part of the complete home energy ecosystem — not bolted on as an afterthought.

## Key Features

### Dynamic Load Balancing
The charger automatically adjusts how much power it draws based on the home's real-time electricity consumption. This prevents circuit overloads while maximising the use of spare solar capacity. The car charges as fast as the available surplus allows.

### Vehicle-to-Home (V2H) Readiness
Solar Surge installs every EV charger with V2H capability in mind. When Vehicle-to-Home technology becomes widely available in Australia, the customer's car becomes a mobile battery — able to power the home during blackouts or peak tariff periods. No new hardware required.

### App Control
- Schedule charging sessions for when solar production is highest
- Monitor solar generation, EV charging draw, and battery status from a phone
- Track savings — see exactly how much free solar fuel the car has received
- Set the car to charge only when solar production exceeds home consumption

### Full Site Assessment
Solar Surge handles the complete installation process:
- Site assessment
- Switchboard upgrades (if required)
- Dedicated EV circuit installation
- Network connection and commissioning

## Objection Handling

### "I don't have an EV yet."

**Plant the seed:** "Totally fine — when you do, you'll be ready. EV prices are dropping significantly across Australia and most analysts expect a major uptake in the next 2–3 years. Having a solar system with a hybrid-ready inverter means when you do make the switch, adding EV charging is straightforward. Our consultant can show you what the full ecosystem looks like in the audit — so you can plan ahead."

---

### "Can I just plug into a normal power outlet?"

**Educate:** "You can, but there are two problems. First, it's very slow — a standard 10-amp outlet can take 30+ hours to charge a full battery. Second, it doesn't integrate with your solar — your EV just draws from the grid whenever it's plugged in. Without smart load balancing, it can also create demand spikes that show up on your electricity bill. A properly integrated smart charger is the difference between charging on free solar and paying grid rates for every kilometre."

---

### "Do I need a battery to charge my EV from solar?"

**Clarify:** "You don't need a battery — but the two work really well together. Without storage, you're limited to charging while the sun is up. With a battery, you can also charge from stored solar in the evening. For most customers we'd show both options in the audit so you can decide what makes sense for your household."

---

### "Will I need an electrical upgrade? Sounds complicated."

**Reassure:** "It's all part of our service — we do the full site assessment including your switchboard. Sometimes an upgrade is needed, sometimes it isn't. Our team identifies that before any work starts, so there are no surprises. You'll know exactly what's involved and what it costs before you commit to anything."

## Knowledge Check

**Q1: What does Dynamic Load Balancing do?**  
A: Automatically adjusts the EV charger's power draw based on real-time home consumption, preventing circuit overloads and maximising solar usage.

**Q2: What does V2H (Vehicle-to-Home) readiness mean?**  
A: The charger is installed ready for Vehicle-to-Home technology — when available, the car's battery can power the home during outages or peak tariff periods.

**Q3: Approximately how much does it cost to drive 100km when charging from solar?**  
A: Approximately $1 per 100km (vs $15–$20 for petrol).

**Q4: Do you need a battery to charge an EV from solar?**  
A: No — but battery storage extends the charging window into the evening. Without storage, charging is limited to daylight solar generation hours.

**Q5: When should you mention EV Solutions on a call?**  
A: After booking the main appointment, always ask if the household has or is considering an EV. It's a natural cross-sell add-on to any product audit.
