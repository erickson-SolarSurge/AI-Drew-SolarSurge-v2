## WA Residential Battery Scheme — Full Explainer

The WA Residential Battery Scheme is a **state government program** administered in partnership with Synergy (metro Perth) and Horizon Power (regional WA). It began on 1 July 2025 and runs until 2027 or until 100,000 households have participated — whichever comes first.

### Rebate Amounts
- **Synergy customers (Perth metro):** $1,300 rebate
- **Horizon Power customers (regional WA):** $3,800 rebate (higher to offset the higher cost of energy in regional areas)
- Applies to the **first 10kWh** of battery storage

### Key Eligibility Conditions
1. Battery must be installed on or after 1 July 2025
2. Battery must appear on the **Clean Energy Council (CEC) approved battery list**
3. **VPP participation is mandatory** — the battery must be enrolled in a Virtual Power Plant program (this is non-negotiable)
4. From 1 May 2026: new technical requirements apply for systems connected to the SWIS (Western Power network)
5. Rebate amount decreases annually from 2026 to 2030
6. Scheme is capped at 100,000 households

### What Is a Virtual Power Plant (VPP)?

A VPP is a network of home batteries that are linked together and can act as a single large energy source when the grid needs support — for example, on extremely hot days when demand peaks.

**For the customer, in simple terms:** Synergy or Horizon Power can occasionally draw a small amount of energy from the battery to support the grid. The home's energy needs are still prioritised first. In exchange for enrolling in the VPP, the customer receives the state rebate.

**On-call explanation:**
> "It basically means your battery is part of a network that helps keep the whole grid stable on really hot days. Your home still gets power first — but occasionally Synergy might draw a small amount from the battery side. In exchange, you get the state rebate. Most customers find it's a very fair trade."

## WA No-Interest Loan — Full Explainer

In addition to the rebate, eligible WA households can access an **interest-free loan** to further reduce the upfront barrier to battery installation.

### Key Details
- Loan amount: **up to $10,000**
- Interest rate: **0% — completely interest-free**
- Eligibility: Combined gross household income under **$210,000/year**
- Must be installing an eligible battery system
- Can be combined with the state rebate AND the federal rebate

### On-Call Approach
Do not confirm specific loan amounts, interest terms, or income thresholds on a call. The details are confirmed during the audit process. On calls, frame it as:

> "There's also an interest-free loan option of up to $10,000 for eligible households — our consultant will walk you through whether you qualify at the free audit."

## Distributed Energy Buyback Scheme (DEBS)

DEBS is WA's solar feed-in equivalent — the rate paid to Synergy customers for solar energy they export to the grid.

Key characteristics:
- Rates vary by time of export: **peak, off-peak, and shoulder** periods
- Managed by Synergy
- Works similarly to feed-in tariffs in other states
- Low export rates (like all Australian states) reinforce the battery pitch — store it rather than export it

| Feature | Synergy | Horizon Power |
|---|---|---|

| WA battery rebate | $1,300 | $3,800 |
| Network | Western Power (SWIS) | Separate regional networks |
| Solar Surge primary area | Perth, Fremantle, Joondalup, Mandurah, Rockingham, Canning Vale | Regional WA customers may also be served |

Most Solar Surge WA customers will be on the **Synergy network**. When calling regional WA prospects, confirm their electricity provider and note the higher Horizon Power rebate as a key incentive.

## Objection Handling — WA Specific

### "I've heard the WA rebate is running out."

**Clarify:** "The WA scheme is capped at 100,000 households and runs until 2027 — but it's first-come, first-served once the cap is reached. And the rebate amount decreases annually from 2026. So waiting does cost money — the audit is the right first step to lock in the current level."

### "Why do I have to join the VPP?"

**Reframe:** "The VPP is a condition of the WA state rebate — it's what allows the government to fund the incentive. Your battery prioritises your home, and the system only occasionally provides small amounts back to the grid. The rebate you receive is typically worth far more than any minor impact on your stored energy. It's genuinely one of the better trade-offs in the industry."

### "My mate got a rebate in QLD — is WA the same?"

**Clarify:** "WA is actually better in some ways. QLD only has the federal rebate at the moment — the state scheme there closed in 2024. WA still has its own state rebate on top of the federal one. So a WA customer can stack both, which is why the combined savings are typically higher than in QLD."

## Knowledge Check

**Q1: What are the WA state battery rebate amounts for Synergy vs Horizon Power customers?**  
A: Synergy (Perth metro): $1,300. Horizon Power (regional WA): $3,800. Both stack on the federal ~30% rebate.

**Q2: Is VPP participation required for the WA battery rebate?**  
A: Yes — mandatory. Not optional. The battery must be enrolled in a Virtual Power Plant program to qualify for the state rebate.

**Q3: What is the combined rebate estimate for a Synergy customer installing a 10kWh battery?**  
A: Approximately $3,820 combined (WA state $1,300 + federal ~$2,520). Do not quote exact figures on calls.

**Q4: What is the income eligibility threshold for the WA no-interest loan?**  
A: Combined gross household income under $210,000/year — up to $10,000 interest-free loan.

**Q5: A prospect says they are on Horizon Power in regional WA. What do you tell them about their rebate?**  
A: They qualify for the higher $3,800 state rebate (vs $1,300 for Synergy customers) — plus the federal ~30% rebate on top. Regional WA customers often have the highest combined incentives in the country.

**Q6: When did the WA Residential Battery Scheme begin and when does it end?**  
A: Began 1 July 2025. Runs until 2027 or when 100,000 household cap is reached — whichever comes first. Rebate amounts decrease annually.
