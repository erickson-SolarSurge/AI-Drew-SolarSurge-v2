## What Is It

Commercial Solar is Solar Surge's offering for Australian businesses. Unlike residential solar — which is sized for a household — commercial systems are custom-engineered around the operational realities of a business: shift patterns, demand charges, peak pricing, and seasonal energy variation.

Every commercial engagement starts with deep financial analysis before any commitment is made.

**Key stats:**
- 30–50% typical reduction in operational energy costs in year one
- Electricity is the second-largest operating expense for most Australian businesses
- 100% in-house licensed commercial electrical teams — full workplace safety compliance
- Custom financial modelling (ROI, payback period, energy cost projections) provided upfront

---

## The Core Problem: Energy Costs Eating Business Margins

For most Australian businesses, electricity is the **second-largest operational cost** after wages — and it's the one they have the least control over. Tariff hikes, demand charges, and peak pricing create a new shock every quarter.

**The key shift in framing:** Commercial solar is not a green initiative. It is a **financial strategy**. The ROI is measurable from day one.

Businesses that eliminate or stabilise their energy costs now will outcompete businesses that continue to absorb rising tariffs over the next decade.

## What Makes Commercial Solar Different From Residential

| Factor | Residential | Commercial |
|---|---|---|
| Load profile | Household appliances, evening peak | Shift patterns, industrial equipment, demand charges |
| System size | Typically 6–20kW | 20kW+ depending on usage |
| Design approach | Standard sizing for household | Bespoke engineering around operational needs |
| Financial modelling | Estimate-based | Detailed ROI, payback period, energy cost projections |
| Compliance | Residential electrical code | Full commercial workplace safety compliance |

## The Three Commercial Differentiators

### 1. Financial Modelling First
Detailed ROI analysis, payback period calculations, and energy cost projections are provided **before the business commits to anything**. Data-driven decisions, not sales pressure.

### 2. Operational Integration
Solar Surge designs the system around the business's actual operations — including:
- Shift patterns and staff hours
- Peak demand periods
- Seasonal energy variation
- Equipment load profiles

The system works with the business, not against it.

### 3. Licensed Commercial Teams
Every commercial installation is carried out by Solar Surge's own licensed commercial electrical teams — not subcontractors. This ensures:
- Full compliance with commercial electrical and workplace safety codes
- Ongoing accountability from the company that designed and installed the system
- No liability gaps from third-party crews

## Who Is This For — Qualifying on Call

**Green flags — right business:**
- Business owner or decision-maker for energy costs
- High monthly electricity bill (particularly businesses operating during daylight hours — warehouses, manufacturing, retail, hospitality)
- Business that operates during the day (solar is most effective for daytime operations)
- Owner-occupied or long-term leased commercial property
- Business focused on cost control and margin protection

**Proceed with caution or re-route if:**
- Business leases short-term — lease agreement may not allow solar installation
- Decision-maker is not available — always target the owner or CFO
- It is a large corporate — Solar Surge specialises in SME commercial, not enterprise

## Objection Handling

### "We lease our building — I can't put solar on it."

**Qualify:** "That's worth checking on the lease terms — some commercial leases do allow solar with landlord approval, especially longer leases where the landlord may also benefit from increased property value. We can help you understand what questions to ask your landlord if you want to explore it. Is it a long-term lease?"

---

### "Our usage varies too much — solar won't work for us."

**Educate:** "That's exactly why we do operational integration modelling. We design the system around your actual load profile — including seasonal variation, peak demand, and shift patterns. It works with how you run. The free audit is specifically designed to show you what solar looks like for your specific business, not a generic template."

---

### "We've had solar quotes before that never came to anything."

**Reframe:** "We hear that a lot. Most commercial solar quotes are just panel counts and prices — they don't tell you what the ROI actually is. Our commercial audit is a full financial model: payback period, energy cost projections, and how the system is engineered to your specific operation. It's a business decision, not a sales pitch."

---

### "Our electricity isn't that high."

**Challenge gently:** "What are you paying per month roughly? *(Get a number)* For a business of your size, we might be able to show you there's more room than you think — especially when we factor in demand charges and peak tariffs which don't always show up clearly on a standard bill. The audit would break that down."
