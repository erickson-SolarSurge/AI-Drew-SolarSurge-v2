## The Core Philosophy

> "We don't sell solar. We engineer energy strategies."

Every customer gets a **free Strategic Energy Audit** — not a sales pitch. Your job as a setter is to book that audit with the right homeowner. The consultant closes, educates, and handles the technical detail. You open the door.

**The four things you must confirm on every call:**
1. They own the property (not renting)
2. They are in our service area
3. Decision-maker is available for the audit (partner/spouse ideally present)
4. There is a genuine need (high bills, existing solar underperforming, wants backup, has EV)
