## What Is It

Energy Upgrade is Solar Surge's battery retrofit service — specifically designed for homeowners who **already have solar panels** but are still experiencing high electricity bills.

This is a very common situation. Solar panels only save money during daylight hours when the sun is generating power and the homeowner is consuming it. Excess generation is exported to the grid for as little as 4 cents per kWh, then bought back at night for 30 cents or more.

Adding a battery closes this gap. Energy Upgrade assesses the existing system, retrofits a premium battery, and integrates it with full blackout protection and live monitoring.

**Key stats:**
- Sell at 4¢/kWh → Buy back at 30¢+ = 650% markup on your own power
- 90%+ self-sufficiency achievable after upgrade
- Blackout protection included as standard
- Works with solar installed by other companies

---

## The Core Problem: Selling Gold for Pennies

> "You're filling up a tank of fuel for free, then pouring most of it down the drain."

Solar panels work hardest during the day. But if the homeowner is at work, at school, or simply not consuming power during peak generation hours, all that energy is exported for 4 cents. At night — when dinner is cooked, the air con runs, and devices are charged — that same energy costs 30 cents or more to buy back.

The fix is not more panels. It is storage. A battery captures daytime surplus and delivers it when it's actually needed.

## Blackout Protection — Key Talking Point

Most solar-only systems shut off during a blackout — this is a legal safety requirement. The customer's panels continue to generate electricity, but it cannot be used because the safety switch disconnects the system from the home.

With an Energy Upgrade battery system, **blackout protection is built-in as standard**. The home stays powered during grid outages. This is a significant selling point for customers in storm-prone areas (QLD) and areas with unreliable grid infrastructure.
