## What Is It

Energy Starter is Solar Surge's entry-level residential solar product — but it is not a budget system. It is a premium-quality solar installation specifically designed to be **battery-ready from day one**.

The critical differentiator: most cheap solar installs use a standard string inverter. When the homeowner eventually wants to add a battery, they must rip out and replace the inverter, costing thousands extra. With Energy Starter, Solar Surge installs a **hybrid-ready inverter** so adding a battery later is plug-and-play — no new inverter, no rewiring.

**Key stats:**
- 70–80% reduction in daytime electricity costs
- $0 wasted on future inverter replacement if battery is added later
- 25-year performance warranty on Tier-1 panels
- ~40% of cheap solar systems underperform within 3 years
- 100% in-house licensed installation — no subcontractors

## The Core Problem: Cheap Solar Is the Most Expensive Mistake

The solar market is flooded with cheap panels and cheap installation crews. The risks:

- Systems that underperform within 2–3 years due to inferior components
- Inverters that fail early
- Warranties that are never honoured (the installer has moved on)
- Non-hybrid inverters that require full replacement when a battery is added

**The principle:** A strategic solar foundation isn't about spending more — it's about **spending once**.

## Who Is This For — Qualifying on Call

**Green flags — right customer:**
- Homeowner with no solar panels currently installed
- Has daytime electricity use (home during the day, retired, works from home)
- Wants to start saving now but isn't ready to invest in a full battery system yet
- Has received a cheap solar quote and is unsure
- Wants to future-proof for when battery prices fall further

**Disqualify or re-route if:**
- They already have solar panels → Route to **Energy Upgrade** (Module 03)
- They want the full battery solution now → Route to **Energy Independence** (Module 01)
- They are renting — confirm ownership: *"Are you the homeowner?"*
