## The Core Problem: The Export Trap

The Export Trap is the central concept for this product. Understand it deeply — it's the hook for most conversations.

**How it works:**
- During the day, solar panels produce electricity. If the homeowner is at work, the excess is exported to the grid.
- Exported energy earns approximately **4 cents per kWh**.
- At night, when the homeowner is actually home using power, they buy electricity back from the grid at **30 cents per kWh or more**.
- That's a **650% markup on their own energy**.

**The result:** Homeowners with solar still get bill shock. The solution is not more panels — it's smarter storage.

> Real example: Magreth from Brisbane received a $6,700 estimated power bill. After Solar Surge's Energy Independence system was installed, her bills dropped by over 90% within one quarter.
