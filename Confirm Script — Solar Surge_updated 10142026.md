# Confirm Script — Solar Surge

Oct 14, 2026 · @Emil Halili

## Why we qualify this strictly

The confirm call is the last check before a consultant drives out, so every appointment must pass all six qualifiers again. Our consultants are independent contractors with no retainer and no fuel pay. A partner who isn't home, a bill under the minimum, a family moving next year, or a roof about to be replaced all mean hours of unpaid driving, and that wrecks morale fast.

Use "what and why" on every question: tell the customer what you're asking and why it matters to them. Be direct and keep it moving.

Never tell a customer that both people attending is a legal or rebate requirement. It isn't one; it's our policy.

## Qualifier quick reference

If any answer lands in "Flag" or "Don't book", don't confirm on the call. Use the holding line at the end and flag to your manager straight away.

## Qualifying questions

Ask these in order and record each answer.

### Q1: Highest power bill

What's the highest power bill you've had in the last 12 months, thinking back to summer with the aircon running? Was that monthly or quarterly? I ask for the highest because summer is when the system works hardest, so that's what the consultant sizes it for.

- Highest bill ($, monthly or quarterly):

*Needs to be $150+ a month or $450+ a quarter.*

### Q2: Both decision-makers there

Our consultant is driving out specifically for you, and they only run the appointment when everyone who owns and lives in the home is there. The system is sized on both your energy habits, and any paperwork or finance needs both of you. So can I confirm that both you and \[partner name\] will be there for the full 45 minutes to an hour?

- Both attending (Y/N):

*Pause. Get a clear yes. If one of them can't make it, rebook to a time that suits both before going any further.*

### Q3: Year of birth

What year were you born? The finance options our consultant can show you have age-based lending criteria, so this makes sure they only bring options you can actually use.

- Year of birth:

*Ask for the year, never the age.*

### Q4: Moving plans

How long are you planning to stay in the home: 0 to 3 years, or 3 years or longer? These systems pay themselves off over a few years, so we want to make sure you're there to get the benefit.

### Q6: Roof and renovations

Last one, and it matters because the panels go on your roof. How's the roof: any leaks, damage, or repairs needed? Any insurance claims open? And is there any big work happening or planned, like a kitchen or bathroom reno?

*If anything comes up, dig in: What's the work? Has it started? When is it booked to finish? Is the roof being repaired or replaced? Has the insurance claim settled, and is it a cash settlement?*
